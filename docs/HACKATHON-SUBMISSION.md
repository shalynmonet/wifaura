**Name:** WIFAURA

**One line:** Unseal daily generative art with WIF, send half to your own
Reserve, and awaken a shared mystery together.

**Categories:** consumer and payments; social and creators.

**What it does:** Each day's sealed entity offers four intentions that shape a
private, reproducible artwork and encounter. Complimentary credits introduce
the ritual. Further encounters cost $1 USD equivalent in WIF on Robinhood Chain,
using a live rate locked into a short-lived quote. A checkout
contract sends half of the payment to the collector's verified Reserve wallet
and half to the creator treasury in one transaction. The app issues the artwork
only after verifying finalized onchain payment evidence.

**Why return:** Each day brings a new sealed encounter. Qualifying unsealings
contribute once per collector per drop toward seven cumulative Collective
milestones. Names and reflections remain hidden until each milestone awakens.
The collector builds a private art vault while the community reveals a shared
story. Reserve delivery is shown as verified payment history, with no promise
of appreciation or financial return.

**Provenance:** Adapted from the owner's MIT-licensed NIMAURA application.
Artwork generation and snapshot replay are preserved. Wallets, WIF checkout,
onchain split verification and deployment state are separate. NIMAURA remains
unchanged.

## Suggested 60-second demo

0–10s: Show WIFAURA's sealed daily artifact and choose an intention.

10–20s: Show verified payer and Reserve wallets, WIF price and 50/50 split.

20–35s: Approve the exact amount, submit payment and show its real explorer
receipt. Cut forward while waiting for actual finality; label the time cut.

35–45s: Open the newly issued artwork in the private Vault and show the
verified Reserve transfer. Recheck the payment to demonstrate no duplicate.

45–55s: Show the Collective Seal and independent milestone progress.
Voiceover: “Every unsealing brings us closer to something greater. Together,
we awaken hidden entities, one collective milestone at a time.”

55–60s: Show the live URL and source link. Use recorded evidence from the
approved production test; do not substitute simulated purchases.
