# WIFAURA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

WIFAURA is a digital collectible application available at https://wifaura.sha-i.com. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit, create an account, or use the application.

## Information we handle

When you sign in with Google, our authentication provider receives basic account information such as your email address, name, and profile image, as provided by Google. Email/password accounts use your email address and authentication credentials. WIFAURA stores account identifiers, profile information, collectible records, selected intentions, and activity needed to maintain your Vault and Collective progression.

If wallet linking and payments are enabled, we handle public wallet and Reserve addresses, ownership signatures, payment quotes, and transaction identifiers to verify ownership and payments. WIFAURA does not need your wallet recovery phrase or private key. Payments are currently disabled.

Our hosting and authentication providers may process technical information, including IP addresses, request details, and security logs, to operate and protect the service. Browser storage keeps sign-in sessions and application state, including payment recovery information when that feature is enabled.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, maintain collectibles and progression, provide the service, verify wallet ownership and payments when enabled, troubleshoot problems, respond to requests, and prevent abuse.

## Providers and disclosure

WIFAURA uses Supabase for authentication and database services, Cloudflare for hosting and delivery, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. These providers process information under their own policies and applicable service arrangements. When enabled, blockchain RPC and pricing providers receive the public addresses or market requests needed for payment verification and quotes. We do not sell your personal information or use Google account information for advertising.

Blockchain transactions and addresses are public. If you transact, the resulting records can be viewed by others and generally cannot be erased from the blockchain. Your Google email is not intentionally written to the blockchain by WIFAURA.

## Retention and requests

Account and collectible records are retained while needed to provide the service. Security, backup, and transaction records may remain where needed for security, recovery, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Public blockchain records remain independently of WIFAURA.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account information, contact aiwithshai@gmail.com from your account email. We may need to verify your identity. Deleting application records cannot delete public blockchain history or records independently maintained by other providers. You can also revoke WIFAURA's Google connection in your Google Account settings.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the application changes, with the revised date shown above. Questions and privacy requests: aiwithshai@gmail.com.
